# House style: Vox-style documentary shorts

The rules every video is checked against. The references behind them are in
`references/vox/` (NOTES.md + frames + sheets). When in doubt, put a frame next to a reference frame.

## Looks (pick per video in the imagery plan; mixing within a video is fine)
| Look | Use it for | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Evidence desk** (default) | facts, places, dates, numbers, documents, connections, screenshots | `mumbai_*` |
| **B. Cinematic still** | emotional moments, people (from behind / silhouetted), "a man walked into..." | `beethoven_*`, `lustig_*` |
| **C. Paper diorama** | products and "how it works" scenes; animated hero shots | `ori_*` |

Suggested default for the user's dark true stories: **A as the base, cutting into B** for scenes
and **C sparingly** for a hero animation.

## Non-negotiables
1. **Real or realistic material, treated as print.** Photos, screenshots, maps and documents are
   printed on paper: halftone or B/W, white cut edge or torn edge, tape corners, paper shadow.
   Nothing is drawn as flat vector shapes (that was the Labubu v1 failure).
2. **One surface per video** (desk paper, or the B/W cinematic world). Same textures throughout.
3. **Muted palette + one accent.** Cream/black/grey + red by default. Mean saturation 0.10-0.30
   (v1 was 0.42).
4. **People:** silhouettes, from behind, out of focus, or historical portraits. No recognisable
   real faces generated by AI. Real people only from real photos the user supplies.
5. **Evidence grammar:** red string + pins, red circles, arrows drawn as marker strokes,
   redaction bars, stamps ("CLASSIFIED", "166 KILLED"), typewriter labels, a red target.

## Motion rules (checked with `scripts/motion_stats.py`)
| Measure | Target | v1 Labubu |
|---|---|---|
| held % (stillness) | 30-60 % (or calm % > 50 for slow-drift B looks) | 5 % |
| busy % | under 15 % | 30 % |
| new composition | every 2-3.5 s (A), longer for B | 0.8 s |
| white flashes | 0 | 4 |

- **Allowed:** slide in from a frame edge and settle (ease-out, no overshoot); tape "slap" (one
  quick settle frame); slow push-in / pan / parallax (2-6 % scale over a shot); rack-focus blur as
  a transition; pieces revealed in sequence on the beat of the narration; marker strokes drawn on.
- **Banned:** spring bounce, overshoot on scale, screen shake (except one hit for a violent
  beat), white flash cuts, constant wobble, rays/sunbursts, confetti.
- **Rhythm:** a new piece or move lands on a stressed word; then hold. Stillness is the tension.

## Typography
- **Narrative text (B):** one serif italic, white, centred, 1-3 words at a time.
- **Labels and stamps (A):** typewriter or condensed slab on paper strips; red stamp boxes.
- **Headlines:** cut-out newspaper strips, or big red serif words (lustig).
- **Captions:** only if the platform needs them; small, same serif, never the Anton-with-outline
  meme style.
- Text is added in Remotion, not baked into AI images (exact wording, timing and easy edits).

## Sound
Drones, low pulses, string swells, impacts on reveals, paper and tape foley (slide, slap, rip),
sparse SFX tied to the story (sirens, a typewriter, a camera shutter). Real music from a library
or Lyria; the synthesized score from v1 is fine only for light pieces. Narration always on top;
music ducked.

## Image prompt style block (reuse verbatim per look, change only the scene)
- **A:** "Archival evidence-desk paper collage: aged cream paper and old newspaper and map sheets,
  hand-cut halftone black-and-white photo cut-outs with white edges, masking tape corners, real
  paper shadows, typewriter labels, muted cream black and grey with a single red accent, print
  grain. NOT 3D, NOT CGI, NOT illustration. Each element a separate piece with a clear edge."
- **B:** "Black-and-white archival photograph, 1900s documentary still, natural window light,
  shallow depth of field, film grain, vignette, figure seen from behind or in silhouette, no
  readable text."
- **C:** "Paper-craft diorama of layered warm grey-beige paper, torn edges, soft studio light and
  real shadows, monochrome with a single {accent} accent."
